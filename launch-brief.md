# MARGINA — Startup Launch Brief

## Recommended Brand

**MARGINA**

Why it wins: short, pronounceable in French and English, directly connected to the high-stakes “margin” decision, and broad enough to extend from neurosurgery into other procedural planning products. It also supports a strong visual system built around boundaries, confidence bands, and the moment a team decides to stop or proceed.

**Tagline FR:** Décider au bon moment, au plus près de la marge.  
**Tagline EN:** Make the right call, at the margin that matters.

Naming risks to verify before launch: trademark clearance in France/EU/UK/US, domain availability, and conflicts with existing medical-device or surgical-navigation marks. Availability has not been verified.

## Positioning

MARGINA is a clinical decision-support company within NEXYTAL Groupe. Its first product is an adjunctive, on-premise software layer for motor-eloquent glioma surgery. It fuses preoperative imaging, intraoperative ultrasound, neurophysiology, physiology, and instrument motion into a continuously updated patient-specific state estimate. At a standardized deep-margin pause, it presents bounded options with benefit, risk, data freshness, and explicit abstention.

MARGINA is not a universal surgery platform, an autonomous surgeon, or an XR hardware company. It is a focused decision layer that makes changing intraoperative evidence easier to interpret without replacing direct mapping, MEP monitoring, surgeon judgment, or standard navigation.

## Offering Architecture

- **Core platform:** MARGINA Twin Engine
- **First product:** Deep Motor Margin decision support for glioma surgery
- **Modules:** multimodal synchronization, deformable registration, uncertainty calibration, action-conditioned forecasting, adaptive display, audit/override logging
- **XR capability:** phase-adaptive visualization with a conventional monitor fallback
- **AI capability:** state estimation, risk forecasting, error detection, abstention
- **Future product family:** MIRA-XR-inspired patient-specific repair planning for mitral procedures, subject to separate clinical, regulatory, and evidence gates

## Commercial Model

Recommended path: enterprise licensing with implementation and training, plus per-procedure or institutional usage options. Early revenue should prioritize pilot deployments, integration services, and strategic partnerships with navigation and ultrasound vendors. Avoid publishing prices until deployment scope, support burden, and regulatory claims are defined.

## Competitive Frame

Traditional approach → static navigation + expert interpretation  
Existing digital solutions → segmentation, visualization, or registration in isolation  
Existing AI solutions → prediction without intraoperative state updates or clear abstention  
Existing XR solutions → immersive visualization without a clinical decision endpoint  
**MARGINA** → a bounded, continuously updated decision layer with confidence release gates and standard-practice fallback

The defensible wedge is not “XR.” It is the combination of one sharp decision, multimodal updating, action-conditioned forecasts, calibrated uncertainty, and a prospective validation path.
