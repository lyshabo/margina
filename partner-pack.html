Résumé partenaire

02

MARGINA en une phrase

Une architecture de recherche proposée pour réunir imagerie, cartographie, physiologie et trajectoires afin d’étudier un état multimodal, la qualité des données, l’incertitude et des comparaisons de scénarios bornées, sans recommander l’action chirurgicale.

Cas de recherche initial proposé

Point de revue à la marge motrice profonde pendant la résection d’un gliome moteur-éloquent, sans remplacer la stimulation directe, les MEP, la navigation ou l’autorité du chirurgien.

Capacités à évaluer

Actualisation peropératoire, prévisions conditionnées par l’action, seuils de confiance, abstention et maintien de la pratique standard restent à développer et à évaluer.

Translation à explorer

Les options d’intégration, de formation, de partenariat et de modèle économique ne seront examinées qu’après les preuves, qualifications et autorisations applicables.

Relation NEXYTAL

MARGINA est un programme en cours de structuration dans l’environnement NEXYTAL ; sa gouvernance juridique, produit, scientifique et de publication reste à confirmer.

Pourquoi étudier maintenant

  • La déformation peropératoire peut réduire la fiabilité d’un modèle préopératoire statique.
  • Des signaux utiles existent, mais leur synthèse au point de revue reste à évaluer.
  • Une recherche responsable exige transparence, supervision humaine, validation externe et gestion du changement.
Document stratégique et de recherche. Ne pas utiliser pour les soins, une décision clinique en direct ou comme preuve d’autorisation réglementaire.

Partner summary

07

MARGINA in one sentence

A proposed research architecture bringing together imaging, mapping, physiology, and trajectories to study a multimodal state, data quality, uncertainty, and bounded scenario comparisons without recommending surgical action.

Proposed initial research use case

A deep motor margin review point during motor-eloquent glioma surgery, without replacing direct stimulation, MEPs, navigation, or surgeon authority.

Capabilities to evaluate

Intraoperative updating, action-conditioned forecasts, confidence thresholds, abstention, and standard-practice retention remain to be developed and evaluated.

Translation to explore

Integration, training, partnership, and business-model options would be considered only after applicable evidence, qualification, and approvals.

NEXYTAL relationship

MARGINA is a program under development in the NEXYTAL environment; its legal, product, scientific, and publication governance remains to be confirmed.

Why study this now

  • Intraoperative deformation may reduce the reliability of static preoperative models.
  • Useful signals exist, but their synthesis at the review point remains to be evaluated.
  • Responsible research requires transparency, human oversight, external validation, and controlled change.
Strategy and research material only. Not for patient care, live clinical decision-making, or evidence of regulatory authorization.

09

Proposed research program for motor-eloquent glioma surgery. Center and participant numbers are conceptual planning ranges, not results from completed studies.

Year 1

Analytical foundation

Common data model, device adapters, retrospective prototype, and formative human factors.

Proposed gate: synchronization, fault detection, and usable representation to be confirmed.
Year 2

Prospective feasibility

Uncertainty calibration and proposed selection of a silent-mode candidate.

Proposed gate: latency, coverage, and critical-defect criteria to be prespecified.
Year 3

Proposed external silent-mode evaluation

One-step forecasts, calibration, prespecified decision utility, abstention, and subgroups; no performance demonstrated.

Approx. 5 proposed centers
200–250 patients: conceptual planning range
Proposed gate: external and subgroup thresholds to be prespecified; configuration to be confirmed.
Year 4

Proposed randomized comparative study

Subject to a sponsor, an approved protocol, and applicable ethics, regulatory, institutional, and site approvals.

8–10 proposed centers
320–400 participants: conceptual planning range
Proposed gate: recruitment, data-quality, and safety criteria to be prespecified.
Year 5

Utility evaluation and translation

Subject to results and approvals: proposed 90-day endpoint, health economics, scientific dissemination, and conditional planning.

Proposed gate: inform a future intended use, subject to applicable qualification and approvals.
