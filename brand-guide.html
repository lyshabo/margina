MARGINA
Mini brand guide · 2026

Margin, signal, judgment.

MARGINA is a clinical decision layer for high-stakes procedural margins. The identity stays precise, calm and operational: one active boundary, one clear decision, no visual noise.

Logo lockup

The crossing mark represents an active boundary and a decision point. Use the full lockup in institutional contexts; use the mark alone for favicon, app icon and XR interface chrome.

Aa Inter

Inter 700 for headlines and labels. Inter 400–500 for body copy and evidence notes.

Usage principles

Use generous whitespace, restrained motion and operational labels. Keep the decision visible. Treat uncertainty as information, not decoration. Maintain a monitor fallback when the context demands it.

Lockup rules

Primary: mark + MARGINA wordmark. Secondary: mark only. Clear space equals the width of the inner crossing. Do not stretch, outline, bevel or place the mark inside a generic rounded badge.
