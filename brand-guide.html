MARGINA
Mini brand guide · Version 1.1
Visual positioning

The margin.
The signal.
The judgment.

MARGINA is the platform brand for high-stakes procedural margins. SAFE-MARGIN-XR names the priority five-year neuro-oncology pathway; MIRA-XR remains a distinct extension. The identity stays precise, calm and operational: one active boundary, one readable choice, no visual noise.

Logo and lockups

Primary

Crossing mark + MARGINA wordmark. Use the mark alone for the favicon, application icon and compact interface elements.

  • Clear space: width of the inner crossing
  • Do not stretch, tilt, bevel or place in a generic badge
  • Light version: Teal on Paper
  • Dark version: Mint or white on Deep

Type and architecture
Inter Aa

Inter 700–800 for headlines and labels. Inter 400–500 for body, data and evidence notes.

  • MARGINA: platform brand and primary lockup
  • SAFE-MARGIN-XR: priority neuro-oncology program
  • MIRA-XR: distinct pathway; no transfer of validation claims
  • Teal: development and validity; Coral: proposed utility study
  • Ranges = conceptual planning, not results
